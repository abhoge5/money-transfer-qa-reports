Quality assurance / Mobile banking

Money transfer recommendations

Prioritized actions for reliable, secure and usable transfers

Recommended priorities

RankDefectRecommendationMeasure of improvement
1 · Highest TC06 / DEF-102MajorTransaction integrity Prevent duplicate debits during retries.Use a persistent idempotency key for each transfer and reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying. Show the user the transaction status instead of inviting a new submission while the result is unknown. Repeated network-loss and retry tests produce exactly one debit; track duplicate debits and reconciliation failures.
2 · Highest TC03 / DEF-101CriticalSecurity and completion Improve OTP delivery without weakening verification.Monitor delivery latency and provider failures; provide a rate-limited resend and an approved secure alternative when delivery fails. Preserve expiration and attempt limits. Track OTP delivery within 60 seconds and verification completion; verify resend limits and expiration in security tests.
3 · High TC15 / DEF-105MajorTransaction success Improve transfer performance under peak load.Profile the 500-user workload, remove the measured bottlenecks, and run repeatable load tests before release. Preserve transaction integrity when requests time out. Meet the existing five-second response-time target at 500 users; track response times, timeouts and completed transfers.
4 · High TC12 / DEF-104MajorError recovery Make beneficiary validation actionable.Show a clear field-level error for an invalid account number, retain the entered data, and allow correction without restarting the transfer. Verify the error is associated with the account field and can be corrected; track validation errors and successful corrections.
5 · Medium TC09 / DEF-103MinorAccessibility and usability Keep the confirmation action accessible on small screens.Adapt the layout and scrolling so the button remains reachable, including with larger text and device keyboard navigation. Verify the confirmation action is visible or reachable on five-inch screens and at supported text sizes.

Ranking considers customer and transaction impact as well as recorded severity. TC13 remains blocked until DEF-102 is fixed; rerun it after the retry fix. Source: test_results.csv and QA test report.
